# Official initial equipment examples

These four equipment designs form the approved initial equipment set. Start with the Circular Recycling System and Armor Plating installed; buy the Auxiliary Fuel Cell and ASECA at a Civilization hub. Rarity indicates scarcity and price band, with no hidden stat multiplier. Basic equipment costs 200–350 Credits, roughly 3–5 average delivery rewards.

| Item | Type | Shape | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Circular Recycling System | Equipment | 2×1 | Common, 200 Credits. Start installed. Every 5 food consumed grants 2 fuel; carries fractional progress; respects equipped fuel cap. |
| Auxiliary Fuel Cell | Equipment | 1×2 | Uncommon, 250 Credits. +20 maximum fuel when installed; no instant refill. |
| Armor Plating | Equipment | 2×1 | Common, 200 Credits. Start installed. +2 maximum armor when installed; no instant repair. |
| Automated Strategic Equipment Control Axis (ASECA) | Equipment | Plus-shaped, 5 cells | Rare, 300 Credits. Once per combat absorbs one projectile; activate with number key 1. |

Equipment can be arranged with cargo in the shared grid. Placement rules must be explicit and shown to the player. Only these four items are in the MVP market; ship purchases/upgrades, an unlock system, energy costs, adjacency requirements and extra durability are not implied.

## Other previously discussed concepts

Circular Recycling System and ASECA are now defined above. Astrasdis Family Decorative Interior remains future cargo content: its metadata, exact shape, rarity and placement rules are not in the MVP baseline. Do not add it to starter content without a separate content pass.
